Privacy policy

MIHM Inbox is a personal tool used only by its owner, on their own computer, with their own Google account.

  • What it accesses: read-only access to the owner's Google Drive, used to read documents in one inbox folder.
  • Where data goes: documents are copied to the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any other person, company or server.
  • Sharing and selling: no data is shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • Credentials: the Google sign-in token is stored in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
